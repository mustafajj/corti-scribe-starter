# SOAP (comprehensive)

## Subjective
The patient presents with 5 days of cough and fever, accompanied by shortness of breath on exertion when climbing stairs. Current medications are metformin 500 mg twice daily and lisinopril 10 mg. The patient is allergic to penicillin.

## Objective
Vital signs: Temperature 38.4. BP 132/84. SpO2 95%.  
Lungs: Crackles right lower lung.

## Assessment
Community-acquired pneumonia.

## Actions and Plan
- Doxycycline 100 mg twice daily for 7 days prescribed due to penicillin allergy.  
- Chest X-ray requested today.  
- Return in 48 hours if not feeling better.
